# AFTER 10 — Prompt per le scene (immagini AI)

Genera 15 immagini (3 verità × 5 momenti) con un generatore di immagini AI a tua
scelta, poi salvale come **WebP verticali 1080×1920** in `public/scenes/` con il
nome file indicato. Se un file manca, il gioco usa lo spioncino vettoriale.

## Stile comune (incolla all'inizio di OGNI prompt)

> cinematic horror still, night, first-person tension, dark low-key lighting,
> desaturated muted palette, deep shadows, 35mm anamorphic, film grain,
> photorealistic, moody, ominous, vertical 9:16 composition, no text, no logo,
> no watermark, no people looking at camera unless specified

## Negative prompt (se il tuo tool lo prevede)

> text, watermark, logo, caption, ui, hud, cartoon, bright, cheerful, colorful,
> oversaturated, extra limbs, deformed, low quality, jpeg artifacts

  from a landing above, a distant human silhouette holding the torch, long
  shadows on the walls.
- **`blk-3.webp`** — a torch beam stopped in front of an apartment door across a
  dark landing, the silhouette of a neighbor knocking at the opposite door, seen
  from inside our darkened hallway.
- **`blk-4.webp`** — flashlight light receding up a dark stairwell, an empty
  landing in the foreground, through a window half the neighborhood is dark,
  blackout night.
- **`blk-5.webp`** — near-total darkness on an apartment landing, almost pure
  black with only the faint circular glint of a door peephole, oppressive
  silence.
